HOW TO GET STARTED

A clear first conversation

You do not need a fully written technical specification to start. A short description of the business goal is enough for an initial enquiry.

01

Describe

Tell us about the business problem, users, expected outcome, and any important deadlines.

02

Discuss

We can review the requirements, clarify open questions, and discuss possible ways forward.

03

Scope

Agree on the appropriate next step, including scope, skills, responsibilities, and timelines where applicable.

04

Plan Next Steps

Determine whether the requirement calls for consulting, solution delivery, specialist talent, or a combination.
